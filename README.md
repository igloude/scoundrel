# Scoundrel

## Weapons

- You may only have one weapon equipped at a time.
- Weapons deal damage equal to their value. When you fight a monster using an equipped weapon, subtract its power from the monster's strike. (5 weapon against a 10 monster, you take 5 damage. If your weapon is stronger than a monster, you take nothing.)
- When you slay a monster with a weapon, you can only slay monsters of lower power with it. The damage never changes, only what it can hit. (e.g. you slay a 7 with a 3 weapon and take 4 damange. Now your weapon can only be used on <= 6 monsters, but will still do 7 damage)
- If you choose a new weapon, you must equip it.
